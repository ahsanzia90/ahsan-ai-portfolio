I'm Ahsan — a product designer who's spent the last ~7 years moving between fintech, security, IoT, hospitality and, more recently, AI-native products. I like problems that don't have an obvious shape yet.

My process starts with the problem, not the UI. Prototyping and design systems are the tools; clarity is the goal. Lately that's meant designing conversational and AI-assisted experiences — including, appropriately enough, the one you're using right now.

Outside of client work, I spend a lot of time thinking about how AI changes the designer's job — not by replacing the craft, but by changing what's worth designing by hand.
